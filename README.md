# 🍫 Nassau Candy Distributor – Profitability Analysis Dashboard

> **Power BI | Data Analytics | Business Intelligence | Sales & Profitability Analysis**

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze the sales performance, costs, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and margin risks of **Nassau Candy Distributor**.

The project transforms raw business data into an interactive analytical solution that helps stakeholders understand **where revenue is generated, where profit is created, and which products may require profitability improvement**.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to develop an interactive profitability analysis dashboard that enables business stakeholders to:

- Monitor overall sales and profitability
- Analyze gross profit and gross margin
- Identify high-performing products
- Compare profitability across divisions and regions
- Analyze sales versus profitability
- Identify products with potential margin risk
- Understand cost contribution
- Support data-driven business decisions

---

## 🏢 Business Problem

A distributor can generate high sales while still having low profitability if product costs and margins are not properly managed.

This dashboard was developed to answer important business questions such as:

- How much revenue is being generated?
- How much gross profit is being earned?
- What is the overall gross margin?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which products have high sales but relatively low margins?
- Which divisions are the most profitable?
- How does profitability vary across regions?
- Which products represent potential margin risk?
- How do costs affect overall profitability?

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| 💰 **Total Sales** | Total revenue generated |

| 📊 **Gross Margin %** | Profitability relative to sales |
| 📦 **Total Units** | Total quantity of products sold |
| 💵 **Total Cost** | Total cost associated with products |

# 🧹 Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared and transformed before developing the dashboard.

Major data preparation activities included:

- Removing duplicate records
- Handling missing/null values
- Checking data types
- Standardizing categorical fields
- Cleaning product and business attributes
- Creating calculated fields
- Creating analytical measures
- Preparing fields for visualization
- Validating analytical data

**Power Query** was primarily used for data cleaning and transformation.

---

# 📐 Data Modeling

The data model was structured to support interactive analysis across different business dimensions.

The dashboard supports analysis by:

- Product
- Division
- Region
- Order Year
- Month
- Sales
- Cost
- Units
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin

The model allows users to dynamically filter the dashboard and analyze profitability from multiple perspectives.

# 📌 Conclusion

The Nassau Candy Distributor – Profitability Analysis Dashboard demonstrates how business data can be transformed into an interactive decision-support solution using Power BI.
The project focuses not only on reporting sales but also on understanding the relationship between sales, cost, gross profit, margins, products, divisions, regions, and profitability risk.
The final dashboard provides stakeholders with an interactive environment for exploring business performance and identifying areas that may require further investigation.
